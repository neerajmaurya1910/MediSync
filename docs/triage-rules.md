# Triage Rules (simplified, educational)

Priority 1 = most urgent ... 5 = least urgent.

| Priority | Any of these vitals |
|---|---|
| 1 | SpO2 < 90, systolic BP < 80, HR > 150 or < 40, RR > 30 or < 8 |
| 2 | SpO2 < 94, systolic BP < 90, HR > 130, RR > 24, temp >= 40 or < 35 |
| 3 | HR > 110, temp >= 38.5, systolic BP > 180, RR > 20 |
| 4 | HR > 100, temp >= 37.8 |
| 5 | Everything else |

Adjustments applied after the vitals check:
- **Keywords** in the complaint set a minimum urgency (e.g. "chest pain" -> at least 2).
- **Age** 1 or younger, or 80 or older, moves the patient one level more urgent.

These thresholds are simplified for learning. Real triage uses validated systems
and trained clinicians.
